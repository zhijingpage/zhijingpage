## Visual design

*In-hand rotation.* is an original Blender concept animation created for this homepage. Five individually animated digits turn an orange calibration block within the robotic hand. The motion was authored for illustration and does not represent a measured laboratory result. Every frame was rendered from the scene without third-party footage.

The site uses `assets/in-hand-rotation.mp4` for playback and `assets/in-hand-poster.webp` for the still preview. The offline source package includes `animation-source/hand.py` and `animation-source/studio.py`.

The page layout, CSS, and 井 identity mark were made for this website. Paper illustrations belong to the respective paper authors. Their original image URLs are recorded in `assets/sources.json`.
